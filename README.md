# CreateKit

Made by Gaurab Pantha for gaurabdev.com.

A free browser toolkit with no account required. Files are processed on your device.

## Tools

- QR code generator
- MP4 to MP3 converter
- Background remover
- Image converter and compressor
- Password generator
- Word counter
- PDF merge and split
- Age and date calculator

## Files

- `index.html` – page layout
- `style.css` – design
- `script.js` – all tool logic

## Notes

The QR, MP3, background remover and PDF tools download their helper libraries on first use. These require an internet connection, but your selected files stay on your device. Large files may take longer or exceed a device's available memory.
